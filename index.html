Questions

Before you book

Is this therapy?

No. These sessions are offered as relationship coaching and emotional support, not psychotherapy, psychiatric care, or medical treatment.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to discuss?

No. You can arrive with a specific question, a confusing relationship situation, or simply a lot on your mind.

Will you tell me whether I should stay or leave?

The goal isn't to make the decision for you. We can explore your situation, needs, boundaries, and options so you can make your own informed decision.

What if I'm experiencing a crisis?

These sessions are not crisis services. If you are in immediate danger or considering harming yourself or someone else, contact your local emergency service or a qualified crisis professional immediately.
